## 1-Click 3-Minute Judge Guided Tour

Click the golden **"Judge Demo Tour"** button in the top banner to launch the 8-step automated sequence:
1. **Command Center Overview**: Inspect top KPIs and regional risk aggregate.
2. **Northeast GIS Map**: Explore 8 layers (Heatmap, NH lifelines, villages, alerts).
3. **XAI Factor Attribution**: Open Sohra or Setijhora zone and inspect the driver waterfall.
4. **Lifeline Road Status**: Review NH-10 BLOCKED status and alternative detours.
5. **Offline Field Reporting**: Simulate offline mode, queue an incident, and reconnect to auto-sync.
6. **CAP v1.2 Early Warning**: Review W3C CAP XML and May 2026 Cell Broadcast payload.
7. **Multilingual Community View**: Switch to Assamese, Bengali, Khasi, or Mizo.
8. **System Telemetry & Audit Log**: Verify tamper-evident decision records.
