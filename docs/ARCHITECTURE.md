## 1. System Positioning & Ecosystem Integration

## 2. Research Facts & Grounded Statistics

1. **National Atlas Exposure**: Approximately 80,000 landslides were mapped by ISRO/NRSC during 1998?2022 across vulnerable regions in 17 states and 2 Union Territories. **~18.8?19% of India's mapped landslides are in the Northeast Himalayas**.
2. **NESAC Seasonal Inventories**: 2025 seasonal inventory reporting documented 374 landslide incidents across the Northeast and 63 reported casualties (non-exhaustive inventory).
3. **Economic Impact**: In 2024, two major pre-monsoon storm events in Nagaland's Peren and Shamator districts caused over ?230 crore in localized losses.
4. **National Dissemination Synergy**: India's indigenous National Cell Broadcast System was launched in May 2026 and integrated with NDMA SACHET. NER-LENS generates compliant upstream CAP v1.2 messages rather than attempting to duplicate telecom dissemination infrastructure.

## 3. Technology Stack Breakdown

| Component | Technology | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Backend Framework** | Python 3.10+ & FastAPI | Async, high-throughput REST APIs with sub-millisecond serialization |
| **Data Validation** | Pydantic v2 | Strict type safety for GIS coordinates, risk drivers, and CAP schemas |
| **Spatial Engine** | In-Memory GeoJSON & Spatial Algorithms | Zero external daemon friction; instant local and cloud deployment |
| **AI Risk Engine** | Deterministic Physics + ML Ensemble Interface | High explainability with transparent factor attribution |
| **Frontend Framework** | React 19 + TypeScript + Vite | Ultra-fast bundling, type safety, modular component architecture |
| **Styling & Theme** | Tailwind CSS v4 | Government-grade design tokens with high-contrast accessibility |
| **GIS Mapping** | Leaflet & React-Leaflet | Lightweight, multi-layer GIS canvas with custom SVG icon markers |
| **Offline Storage** | IndexedDB via idb-keyval & Service Worker | Resilient field operations during Himalayan connectivity disruptions |
| **Multilingual Engine** | Custom i18n Localization Layer | Full support for 7 Northeast regional languages |
